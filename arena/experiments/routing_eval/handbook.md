# IOTE community handbook

This is the handbook for the IOTE Discord server. It's synthetic: written for the routing evaluation (arena/experiments/route_eval.py) and loaded only into a copy of the arena database, never into a real server.

## Server rules

1. Be decent. No slurs, harassment or dogpiling. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
2. No NSFW anywhere except #after-dark, which needs the 18+ role.
3. No self-promotion, except in #promo on Fridays (one post per person per Friday).
4. Tag spoilers with || || for two weeks after a game, film or episode comes out.
5. English in #general. Every other language is welcome in #international.
6. Don't DM members you don't know to trade, sell or recruit. Use #trading.
7. Moderators' calls are final in the moment. Appeals go through the form pinned in #appeals, and Halden reviews them within 3 days.

## Roles

- Verified: react with ✅ in #welcome and answer the three onboarding questions. It can take up to 10 minutes to be granted. You need it to post outside #welcome.
- Regular: granted automatically after 30 days in the server and 200 messages.
- Event Host: apply with the form pinned in #events. You need the Regular role first.
- 18+: ask a moderator in #modmail. Not given on request in DMs.
- Moderators: Kestrel (lead moderator), Bramble and Juno. The admin is Halden.

## Events

All event times are in UTC. Use Discord timestamps or the /time command to see them in your own timezone.

- Game night: every Friday at 20:00 UTC in the Game Night voice channel. The game rotates; vote for next week's in #events by Wednesday 23:59 UTC.
- Movie night: the first Sunday of each month at 19:00 UTC on the Watch Party stage.
- Monthly tournament: the last Saturday of each month at 17:00 UTC. Sign-ups close the Thursday before at 23:59 UTC. At most 32 players, single elimination, best of 3, with a best-of-5 final.

## Giveaways

You must be Verified and have been in the server for at least 14 days to enter. One entry per person. Winners have 48 hours to claim the prize, or it's re-rolled.

## Clips

Post clips in #clips-and-highlights. At most 60 seconds, with the game's name in the title. The clip of the month wins a month of Nitro Basic.

## Emoji

Suggest emoji in #emoji-suggestions as a 128×128 PNG. A suggestion with 10 👍 reactions gets added.

## Levels

You earn XP for chatting, at most once a minute, and 5 XP for every 10 minutes in a voice channel.

## Partnerships

Servers with at least 500 members can ask about partnering. Contact Halden.

## About the server

IOTE was founded on March 14, 2021. The server's birthday event is on March 14 every year.
